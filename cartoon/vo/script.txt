The package was delivered tomorrow.
But it was already sitting on his doorstep today.
Inside was one photo of something that hasn't happened yet.
Him, on this porch, soaking wet, holding this exact box.
Tomorrow. 7:02 PM.
So he did the one thing that would break it.
He waded into a lake and threw the box as far as he could.
He got home at 7:01. The porch was empty.
Then his phone buzzed. One new delivery.
He opened the door, and there it was. Dripping wet.
And the handwriting on the label was his own.
