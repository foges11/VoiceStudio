# MOB RULE: the crowd controls the city

**Name:** Mob Rule (series), "Mob Rule: Day N" (titles). Handle ideas: @mobrule.city, @mobrulecity.

**One-line pitch:** a tiny city of 100 people and their mayor. Every day, the most-liked comment happens to them,
no matter what.

The look: the Brand City template (realistic 3D city, calm camera moves, phrase captions), plus little citizens
and Mayor Bob.

## Why this beats Brand City (from Episode 0's data)

| Episode 0's problem | Mob Rule's fix |
|---|---|
| 43.5% skip rate: the opening looked like a game ad | Something happens in frame 0 (a shadow over the city, people looking up) with a character on screen |
| A cliff at 7–9 s when the rules started | One rule, one line: "Top comment happens." |
| 0 shares, 0 saves | Commenters' names go on citizens; people share to say "my comment did this" or "I survived" |
| Needed brands, which don't reply | Needs anyone with an opinion; every viewer can play |
| 21 s, average watch 8 s | 15 s, with the payoff before 8 s from Day 2 on |
| No story or character | Mayor Bob, plus a population counter that can hit 0 |

## The format (every episode from Day 2)

1. **0–1 s:** yesterday's top comment, with the commenter's @, already happening (the meteor is mid-fall).
2. **1–8 s:** the spectacle and the consequences. Population ticks down (or up). Mayor Bob reacts.
3. **8–12 s:** a twist that sets up a choice: "The survivors want revenge" / "Bob found a button".
4. **12–15 s:** "Top comment decides Day N+1." Ends on a frame that matches frame 0, so it loops.

## The rules (said once, never explained)

- The most-liked comment at posting time + 24 h happens. Ties go to the earliest.
- Commenters become citizens: their @ goes on a house. If a disaster hits their street, they're out.
- Population starts at 100. It can grow (new commenters, good comments) or shrink (disasters). At 0, the season ends.

## What happens when people die

- **Population can grow:** each day, new commenters move in as citizens (their @ on a house), and good comments add
  people too (a hospital, a baby boom). If more people comment than die, the city grows.
- **No disaster wipes out everyone at once:** a disaster hits one part of the city (about 10–40%). The second
  most-liked comment can be a rescue ("Bob builds a shield"), so rescuers compete with destroyers.
- **If it hits 0, that's the season finale:** "Day N: The comments destroyed this city." The last citizen, Bob alone
  on the steps, a memorial wall with every citizen's @ (the most shareable episode), and a villain statue for the
  commenter who finished it off.
- **Season 2 rebuilds from the ruins:** the same city in ruins, Bob survives (he never dies; he's the show's face),
  new citizens move in, and the crowd decides whether to rebuild or repeat history.

## Variety (so it never repeats)

- Disasters: meteor, flood, giant cat, tornado, alien abduction, lava.
- Good stuff: a stadium, a mall, a festival, a statue of the top commenter.
- Chaos: elect a new mayor, ban something, make everyone wear hats.
- Balance: if three disasters win in a row, Bob gets a "veto" episode (a vote between two options).

## Cadence and growth

- **Post daily** at the same time. The comments window closes at posting time.
- **Reply** to the top 10 comments within the first hour.
- **Pin** "Top comment decides Day N+1. Your @ goes on a house."
- **Every 5th episode:** a recap with population, best moments and the most-hated commenter.
- **Cross-post** to YouTube Shorts and Instagram. Count both, and show which platform won.
