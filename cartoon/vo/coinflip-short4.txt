How did twenty-four thousand dollars beat eighty-four thousand? And why are you the one who lost?

Your friend invests two hundred a month. From twenty to thirty. Then stops. Forever.

You start at thirty. Same two hundred. And you never stop.

At sixty-five, who has more?

Your friend. Almost four hundred thousand.

You? Three hundred sixty.

You put in sixty thousand more. And still lost.

Because money needs time to grow. And your friend gave it ten extra years.

So start now. Even small.

And if that sounds crazy, watch it again.
