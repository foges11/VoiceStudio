Stop. Who did you tell your dream to? They might be the reason you never start.
And no, it's not the haters.
Sure, your mom said, "Be careful."
Your uncle said, "My buddy tried that. Lost everything."
Your coworker said, "lol." Out loud.
Those hurt. Your brain remembers one "be careful" over five compliments.
But the person who really kills your dream is the one you'd never suspect.
The one who said, "Oh my god, you're gonna be huge."
In a 2009 study, people whose goals got noticed by others actually did less.
Being noticed gave them a premature sense of already being that person.
You got the reward. Without doing the work.
So build it quiet. Share results, not plans.
Now think again. Who did you tell?
