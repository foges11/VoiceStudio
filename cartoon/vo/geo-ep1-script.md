# Geo Episode 1: I Deleted Every Country Until Only 1 Was Left

Host = only voice. `> BUBBLE` lines are on-screen speech bubbles, never spoken.
Spoken text already uses words for numbers (CapCut-safe).

## Hook

One hundred ninety-five countries. Eight rules. Break one, and you're deleted. And the winner? It's going to make you angry.

Hi. I'm the host. I'm a globe. Which means every country on Earth has been living on me, rent free, for thousands of years. Today, I'm collecting.

Here's how it works. Every round, I read one rule. If your country breaks it, you're gone. No appeals. No crying. Okay, crying is allowed. It just won't help.

## Cast

Let's meet the ones you actually care about.

America. Big hat. Bigger ego. Already asking where the prize is.

Canada. Apologized for being here.

Russia. The biggest country on Earth. Will remind you.

Brazil. Came here for a party.

The United Kingdom. Brought a teacup to a death match.

France. Thinks it already won.

Japan. Read the rules before I wrote them.

Australia. Everything on Australia wants to kill you. Including Australia.

Italy. Hasn't said a word. Just hand gestures.

Germany. Brought a clipboard. Asked if the rules are in writing.

And the Vatican. The smallest country on Earth. You can walk across the whole thing in under twenty minutes.

Find your country. Put it in the comments right now. Let's see how long it lasts.

## Round 1: The Equator

Rule one. If the Equator runs through your country, you're deleted.

The Equator is the imaginary line around my waist. And yes, I'm sensitive about it.

Thirteen countries sit on that line. Ecuador, which is literally named after it. Colombia. Kenya. Indonesia. Somalia. And...

> BUBBLE (Brazil): Wait, I just got here!

Brazil. Showed up dressed for a party. Party's over.

And Ecuador named itself after the line that just deleted it. That's like getting a tattoo of your ex.

## Round 2: The Stans

Rule two. If your country's name ends in "stan," you're deleted.

Afghanistan. Pakistan. Kazakhstan. Uzbekistan. Turkmenistan. Kyrgyzstan. Tajikistan. Seven countries. Gone.

Fun fact. "Stan" comes from Persian, and it means "place of." So Afghanistan is "place of the Afghans." Which means if America followed the rules, it would be called Burgerstan.

> BUBBLE (USA): Hey!

Nobody from our cast got hit. America still checked its name twice.

## Round 3: Landlocked

Rule three. If your country has no coastline, you're deleted.

Forty-four countries have no coast at all. Switzerland. Austria. Hungary. Mongolia. And Bolivia.

Here's the thing about Bolivia. Bolivia used to have a coast. It lost it to Chile in a war in the eighteen hundreds. And Bolivia still has a navy. A navy. With no ocean. It trains on lakes and rivers. That's not a navy. That's a boat club with commitment.

And our first big name goes down. The Vatican. No coast. Completely surrounded by one city. Rome.

> BUBBLE (Vatican): I'll pray for all of you.

> BUBBLE (Italy): Bye, roommate.

## Round 4: No Red

Rule four. If your flag has no red on it, you're deleted.

Sweden. Greece. Argentina. Finland. Ukraine. Blue, yellow, white. Beautiful flags. Gone.

Oh, and Brazil. Green, yellow, blue, white. No red. Brazil just got deleted twice.

> BUBBLE (Brazil, ghost): I WASN'T EVEN HERE!

Everyone else in our cast? Red. Every single flag. America, Canada, Russia, the UK, France, Japan, Australia, Italy, Germany. All safe.

Turns out the secret to survival is just red. Write that down, Germany.

> BUBBLE (Germany, writing): Red. Noted.

## Round 5: No Neighbors

Rule five. If your country doesn't share a land border with any other country, you're deleted.

Japan. Over fourteen thousand islands. Not one of them touches another country. Gone.

> BUBBLE (Japan, bowing): Thank you for having me.

Australia. Gone.

> BUBBLE (Australia): I'm not an island, I'm a CONTINENT!

Sure. A whole continent, and not one neighbor. That's not a flex. That's a red flag.

And the United Kingdom. An island. Pack it up.

Except, wait. Northern Ireland. Northern Ireland shares a land border with Ireland. About five hundred kilometers of it.

So the UK survives. Thanks to the one border it has spent a hundred years arguing about.

> BUBBLE (UK): Jolly good.

## Round 6: The Crown

Rule six. If your country has a king, a queen, or an emperor, you're deleted.

> BUBBLE (UK): Oh, come ON.

The United Kingdom. Survived on one border. Lasted one round. King Charles. Gone.

And Canada. Canada's king is... also King Charles. He's king of fifteen countries. That's not a king. That's a subscription.

> BUBBLE (Canada): Sorry.

Japan, by the way, has the oldest monarchy in the world. So Japan, you were going home either way.

> BUBBLE (USA): I fought a whole war so I wouldn't have a king.

And it worked. America is still in.

## Round 7: Too Many People

Rule seven. If more than one hundred million people live in your country, you're deleted.

India. China. Indonesia. Nigeria. Mexico. Gone.

America. About three hundred forty million people. Gone.

> BUBBLE (USA): Objection!

Overruled. I'm a globe. You live on me.

And Russia. The biggest country on Earth. About one hundred forty-four million people. Gone. Russia stretches across eleven time zones, and it still ran out of time.

> BUBBLE (Russia): ...

## The Final

Three left. France. Germany. Italy.

Final rule. Whoever borders the most countries wins. Everyone else, deleted.

Italy, you're up. France. Switzerland. Austria. Slovenia. San Marino. And the Vatican. Six.

> BUBBLE (Italy): The Vatican still counts! It lives at my place!

It counts. You still lose. Italy, deleted.

Germany. Germany has a clipboard. Denmark. Poland. Czechia. Austria. Switzerland. France. Luxembourg. Belgium. The Netherlands. Nine. Nine neighbors.

> BUBBLE (Germany): Efficient.

France. Belgium. Luxembourg. Germany. Switzerland. Italy. Monaco. Andorra. Spain. Eight.

Germany wins. It's over. Germany followed every rule, did all the paperwork, and...

Wait. France isn't done.

France still has land all over the world. Like French Guiana, in South America. Which borders Suriname. And Brazil.

Yes. Brazil. Deleted twice. And it turns out Brazil is France's longest border.

> BUBBLE (Brazil, ghost): I'M BACK!

And on one tiny Caribbean island, Saint Martin, France shares a border with the Netherlands.

Eleven. France has eleven neighbors. France wins.

> BUBBLE (France, crowned): Obviously.

## Outro

I told you the winner would make you angry. Germany did everything right. France won by never fully going home.

Now be honest. Which round would your country have died in? Tell me in the comments.

And next episode, the countries get a vote. And they're voting on me.

- Charles III: king of 15 Commonwealth realms (UK + Canada among them). Japan: oldest continuous hereditary monarchy.
- Populations: USA ~340M, Russia ~144M (UN est.). Russia spans 11 time zones.
- Italy neighbors: 6. Germany: 9. France: 8 in Europe + Brazil, Suriname (French Guiana) + Netherlands (Saint Martin) = 11. France-Brazil (~730 km) is France's longest land border.
