## What each does well

- **how really?** ("Why $2 Trillion vanishes every year" 1.2M, "The Dark Psychology of Super Rich People" 1.1M,
  "Every Way to Get Rich Explained in 14 Minutes" 630K). Only 11 long videos and 25K subscribers, yet the typical
  video gets 3.6x its subscriber count. Proof that topic + title + thumbnail beat channel size. Moves: "dark
  psychology", a huge specific number, "every X explained in N minutes".
- **Antidote** ("The Psychology of Money in 33 minutes" 4.1M, "The Laws of Human Nature in 50 Minutes" 3.2M).
  Rides demand that already exists: famous books people search for, "in N minutes", long videos (30–50 min) = huge
  watch time.
- **Better Than Yesterday** ("How I Tricked My Brain To Like Doing Hard Things" 28M, "Why You Can't FOCUS - And How
  To Fix That" 5.3M). Simple stick-figure animation, a problem everyone has, the brain science, then the fix. 11–14 min.
- **Better Ideas** ("The fastest way to ruin your entire life" 7.2M). Bold, slightly dramatic titles about your own
  life; short punchy essays.
- **Two Cents** ("How 'Learn to Code' Backfired on a Whole Generation" 607K, "The WORST Financial Advice on the
  Internet" 316K). Contrarian money takes; "backfired", "worst", "is X really a thing?".
- **The Swedish Investor** ("The Intelligent Investor's Road to $1,000,000" 5.3M, "Warren Buffett: 12 Mistakes Every
  Investor Makes" 2.1M). Famous names + a big number or a list.
- **EverythingProfessor** ("Every Rare DNA Trait You Might Have Without Knowing" 1.2M). The "every X" compilation
  format: one video, many mini-hooks, easy to clip.
- **Psych2Go** (13.2M subscribers, but the typical video gets 67K). A warning: big subscriber counts don't mean the
  current format works. Don't copy it.

## The shared recipe

1. **The viewer's own life is the topic:** your brain, your money, why you're broke.
2. **Borrowed demand:** famous books (The Psychology of Money), famous people (Buffett), or questions people already
   search ("why am I always broke").
3. **Specific, dramatic packaging:** a big number ($2 trillion, $1,000,000), "dark psychology", "every X explained",
   "backfired", "worst".
4. **Simple animation is enough:** stick figures (Better Than Yesterday), clean motion graphics (how really?,
   Antidote). The writing carries it, not the art.
5. **Length 10–15 min** for most; 30+ min works only for book summaries.
