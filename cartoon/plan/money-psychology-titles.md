# Money psychology: title research and 20 video ideas

## What the viral titles do (data, not opinion)

From 436 recent long-form videos on 11 animated self-improvement, psychology, money and economics channels.
"Breakouts" are the 78 videos at 2x or more of their own channel's median views; "underperformers" are the 94 at
0.6x or less.

| Title feature | Breakouts | Underperformers | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Says "you"/"your" | 29% | 19% | The clearest difference: talk to the viewer about their own life |
| Two-part title (dash, colon or brackets: the problem, then the fix or twist) | 36% | 27% | "Why You Can't FOCUS - And How To Fix That" |
| First person ("I", "my") | 8% | 2% | Rare, but "How I Tricked My Brain…" was the biggest outlier (78x). Only use it if it's true for you |
| Starts with Why/How/What | 38% | 36% | Common everywhere: table stakes, not an edge |
| Contains a number | 17% | 15% | Small edge; specific numbers ($1,000, 7 signs) beat round ones |
| Named person, brand or place | 96% | 87% | Concrete nouns beat abstract ones |
| Median length | 48.5 chars / 8 words | 44 chars / 8 words | Breakouts are slightly longer, because the two-part titles carry more |

**Honest read:** titles separate breakouts from duds only a little. The topic and the thumbnail do most of the
work. What the breakouts consistently do is make the viewer the subject ("you"), name a problem they have, and promise
either the reason or the fix.

### The patterns worth copying (title shapes, not wording)

1. **Why [your problem] - And How To Fix It**: "Why You Can't FOCUS - And How To Fix That" (15x)
2. **How I tricked my brain into [hard thing]**: "How I Tricked My Brain To Like Doing Hard Things" (78x). First person only if true
3. **Use [a weakness] to your advantage - The [named] Rule**: "Use Laziness To Your Advantage - The 20 Second Rule" (19x)
4. **[N] Signs You [unflattering thing]**: "7 Signs You Might Be Less Intelligent Than You Think" (3x)
5. **[Common belief] doesn't work. Here's why**: "Vegan diets don't work. Here's why" (3.1x)
6. **How [named person or company] [did something huge or terrible]**: "How Soros Made A Billion Dollars And Almost Broke Britain" (4.1x)
7. **The $[number] [thing] doing [something] to your brain**: "The $212 Billion Dollar Food ingredient poisoning your Brain" (3.5x)
