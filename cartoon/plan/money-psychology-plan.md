# Money psychology channel: plan

**Lane:** animated explainers on why people do what they do with money. Room to widen into general psychology later
without changing the channel's name or look.

**Rhythm:** one long video a week (8–10 min), plus three Shorts/Reels cut from each (each with its own fresh hook).

## Channel name ideas (check the handle is free on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok)

- The Money Brain
- Brain Budget
- Spent (short, punchy, fits a later move into general psychology less well)
- Mind Money

## Style

- Simple 2D motion graphics (like the Equal Money video): faster to make and render than 3D, and easy to read on a
  phone. One recurring character (a stand-in for "you") makes it a show, not a slideshow.
- Every video answers one question. The first 30 s states it, proves the payoff exists and starts answering.
- Never invent a number. Every figure on screen has a source in the description.

Recommended first upload: #1. It's concrete, true, easy to animate, and has three standalone clip moments: the
"$9.99 feels like $9" reveal, the experiment showing prices ending in 9 sell more, and how to beat the trick.

## Clip plan (every video)

- Pick three moments that make sense alone: a surprising fact, a mini-story, a "try this" tip.
- Record a new first line for each clip (score it with /ig-reel or /yt-script).
- End each clip with a reason to watch the full video ("the full video has the experiment that proves it").

## Before each video

1. Research the topic and collect sources (real studies and figures only).
2. Write the script with /yt-script: five hooks, scored, keep the best.
3. Write titles and the thumbnail with /yt-package; the description with /yt-seo.
4. After posting, read the retention export with /yt-retention and fix the next video.
